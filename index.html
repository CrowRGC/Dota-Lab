SOURCES & SCOPE

Understand what’s behind each tool.

This is a community planning workspace for Dota 1. The tools explain their assumptions and keep editorial draft advice, calculated scenarios and map annotations separate.

Hero Suggestions · curated advice

The catalog covers 112 classic heroes. Positions and ability tags are editorial guides for finding team needs; they are not measured win rates or a complete verification of the current map’s abilities. Confirm availability and recent ability changes in your client.

Build Duel · automatic map stats

Choose a hero, level and items. Base attributes, natural growth, damage, armor, HP, mana, HP/MP regeneration and passive item bonuses are calculated from tables extracted from the exact v7.07b1 map. Compare two builds on one hero, change one item, or use Hero vs Hero with independent heroes and levels. The latter shows physical hits into the opposing defenses, poison/burn DPS, ready procs and explicitly enabled active effects separately. It does not simulate a whole fight or predict a winner.

The automatic model currently covers 112 heroes and the full named item registry, including recipes and toggle/charge variants. Learned skills, talents, temporary buffs and listed special effects are excluded. Some scripted or aura-based items explicitly mark automatic totals as incomplete until their special effects are modelled. Advanced manual stats remain available for custom scenarios.

The developer’s 7.00 changelog documents the modern positive-armor reduction formula. Matching constants appear in the target map. Negative armor in that model requires a manually entered physical-resistance percentage. Calculated base stats have not yet been checked against a fresh gameplay session.

Strategy Board · the actual map asset

The board uses the target map’s default tactical minimap from its required official texture archive. The original 256 × 256 image is enlarged for display. Place markers, draw routes and arrange strategy steps. Its ward-vision preview uses extracted terrain and tree elevations; it is an approximation, not Warcraft III’s exact visibility engine. Custom blockers, cut trees and dynamic game events are not fully simulated.
